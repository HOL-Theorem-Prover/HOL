# Checked cryptographic curve instances

These theories instantiate the generic short-Weierstrass group library over
named prime and polynomial-quotient fields. They specify mathematical groups;
they do not implement optimized arithmetic, serialization or pairing evaluation.

## Prime-field curves

* `secp256k1Instance`, `p256Instance`, `bn254G1Instance`, `bls12381G1Instance`:
  standard curve parameters and generators, nonsingularity, membership, exact
  prime generator order, and generated prime-order subgroups.
* `cofactorOneInstances`: full cardinalities and cofactor one for secp256k1,
  P-256 and BN254 G1; identifies their generated subgroups with the full groups.
* `secp256k1Endomorphism`: the cube-root coordinate map and its scalar eigenvalue,
  including the equality on all curve points using the cofactor-one result.
* `bls12381Cardinality`: the full BLS12-381 G1 count and full cofactor, using
  independent certificate subgroups rather than assuming a single full-order
  generator exists.

## Twists and extension-field embeddings

* `pairingTwistInstances`: BN254 G2 with coefficient `3/(9+i)` and BLS12-381 G2
  with coefficient `4+4*i`; standard generators and their exact prime orders.
* `pairingTwistMorphisms`: injective twist embeddings into the degree-12 curve
  groups, preserving scalar multiplication and generator order. These are not
  surjectivity claims and do not define a pairing function.
* `bn254TwistCardinality`: full BN254 G2 cardinality, a full-order certificate
  point, full cofactor, and cofactor-clearing results.
* `bls12381TwistCardinalityCertificates`: checked scalar/field-power and CM
  inputs to the BLS12-381 G2 count.
* `bls12381TwistCardinality`: the full BLS12-381 G2 cardinality and full cofactor
  derived from the certificate theory and independent subgroup factors.

## Interfaces and trust boundary

`*_group` denotes all rational points of the curve over its stated field;
`*_subgroup` is generated by the named standard point. Exact generator order
alone does not establish the full group cardinality. For BLS12-381 G1 and both
G2 twists, the standard prime-order subgroup is proper.

Full cofactors here are full curve cardinality divided by the scalar prime,
not effective hash-to-curve cofactors. The general G2 clearing conclusions
place a curve point in scalar-prime torsion; they do not automatically identify
that torsion with the named generated subgroup or prove the result nonzero.

Coordinates, inverse candidates and scalar milestones are untrusted data.
The [certificate libraries](../certificates/README.md) construct HOL proofs.
Prime parameters and cofactor-prime theorems are reused from the existing
`examples/algebra/primality/examples` theories, without duplicate Lucas tables.
Field definitions and embeddings come from
[`field_certificates`](../../field_certificates/README.md).
Quadratic and degree-12 field elements are canonical ascending-coefficient
polynomial lists; zero is `[]`, not `[0]`.

## Native build

Run from this directory with a configured Poly/ML HOL installation using a
CV-capable standard or experimental kernel:

```sh
/path/to/HOL/bin/Holmake -j2
```

The complete build includes the long full-cardinality certificates. To build
just one instance and its dependencies:

```sh
/path/to/HOL/bin/Holmake secp256k1InstanceTheory.uo
```

The complete package is selected explicitly at selftest level 3, separately
from the generic library's level-1 coverage. It depends on the generic curve
and field/certificate packages; no source from the legacy `examples/elliptic`
hierarchy is imported.
